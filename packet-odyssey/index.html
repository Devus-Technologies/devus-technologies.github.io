Packet Odyssey

Learn computer networking by building your own. Drag computers, phones, hubs, switches, routers, modems and Wi-Fi access points onto floating islands, pull the right cables, give each device an address, then send a ping and watch Paquet, a friendly envelope, travel across your network, step by step.

Privacy policy Contact and support

The game

  • A campaign of 60 levels in 13 chapters, from the first cable to cyber defense and the Internet.
  • A sandbox, a level editor, a subnet calculator and a mini command line.
  • Available in 32 languages, playable fully offline, calm music and soft sound effects you can switch off.
  • For players aged 13 and over. No account, no sign-in.

Privacy at a glance

  • No account. We never ask for your name, e-mail address, location or contacts.
  • Progress, networks and levels stay on your device.
  • Anonymous statistics and crash reports are sent only if you agree (Settings > Privacy).
  • Until you have finished 3 levels or played 20 minutes, the game sends nothing and shows no ads.
  • Full details: privacy policy.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob). Ads never appear during a level or in the editor, and their content is limited to what is suitable for teenagers. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy and ad choices. You can also reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Help

  • Move my progress to another phone: Settings > Backup > Export to a file, then Import from a file on the new phone.
  • Delete my data: uninstall the game or clear its storage in the Android settings. Nothing is linked to an account.
  • Change the language: Settings > Language, or the language of your phone.
  • A bug, a question, a data request: write to erci.david.anderson@gmail.com.

Packet Odyssey

Apprends les réseaux informatiques en construisant les tiens. Glisse des ordinateurs, des téléphones, des hubs, des switchs, des routeurs, des modems et des points d'accès Wi-Fi sur des îles flottantes, tire les bons câbles, donne une adresse à chaque appareil, puis envoie un ping et regarde Paquet, une enveloppe amicale, traverser ton réseau pas à pas.
